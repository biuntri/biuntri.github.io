## About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how **Minecraft-server-biuntri** (the "App") handles your information.
The App is developed and operated by the individual developer "biuntri" (the "Developer").

## Purpose of the App

The App uploads backup files of a Minecraft server to your own Google Drive and manages them, for example by deleting old backups.

## Data the App accesses

The App requests only the following Google Drive API scope:

- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`

With this scope, the App can access only the files in your Google Drive that were created by the App (or that you explicitly open with it).
The App does not access any other files in your Google Drive.

The backup files may contain data from the Minecraft server, such as player names, UUIDs, and other identifiers, and server logs that may include IP addresses and chat messages.
The Developer does not receive the contents of these files; the App only sends them to your own Google Drive.

## How data is stored

- The OAuth tokens (credentials) that the App receives from Google are stored only on the machine where you run the App. The App exchanges them only with Google, to authenticate and to call the Google Drive API.
- The Developer does not operate any server that receives your data, and does not collect or store your data.
- Backup files are stored in your own Google Drive.

## Data sharing

- The App does not share, transfer, or sell your data to third parties.
- The App does not use your data for advertising.
- The App does not use your data to develop, improve, or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

## Limited Use

Minecraft-server-biuntri's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access and deleting data

- You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at [https://myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- You can delete the stored OAuth tokens from the machine where you run the App.
- You can delete the backup files from your Google Drive.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.
